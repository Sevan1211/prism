PRISM privacy notice · Updated September 7, 2026

Reading on this device

You can use PRISM without an account. In the browser library, your PDFs, extracted text, lessons, folders and reading positions are stored on your device. Clearing browser data can remove them. Opening the website still sends ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to our host, Cloudflare.

Accounts and optional cloud storage

Clerk manages sign-in, account details, passwords and connected login providers. PRISM receives your account identifier and session information to authorize access to your library. Signing in does not automatically copy your existing browser library or grant an AI agent access.

When you choose a cloud library, its PDFs, lessons, folders, reading state and saved versions are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage and D1 metadata. New changes in that library sync automatically. Each beta account has 1 GB including retained versions and unfinished uploads. Data is protected in transit and at rest, but is not end-to-end encrypted: PRISM’s service can read cloud content.

Working with an agent

Your chosen AI provider is separate from PRISM. Adding a PDF enables your connected agent to read requested source excerpts and page images on that browser under its provider’s terms. The import dialog discloses this before you add the file. You can revoke private-source access in the source overview. Signing in or restoring a cloud library does not transfer these browser-specific grants. Plans and revisions still require your approval. Do not share a source unless you have permission to do so.

Retention, deletion and downloaded copies

Removing an individual source does not immediately erase retained cloud revision history. Use “Delete cloud library” in Storage to remove all cloud files and saved history. Deleting your Clerk account also queues its library for removal through an hourly check; provider outages or large deletions may delay completion. Downloaded copies remain on their devices until you remove their browser data. Signing out does not erase downloaded files.

PRISM retains minimal deletion state to prevent stale devices from restoring a deleted library. Routine application errors do not intentionally include PDF text, passwords or session tokens. Hosting and identity providers process operational and security data under their own policies.

Questions

PRISM is maintained by Sevan Lewis-Payne. Report a privacy issue through the project repository; do not post private PDFs, passwords, tokens or personal account details in public issues.
