PRISM public beta

Updated September 7, 2026

A workspace for your reading

PRISM lets you read PDFs, create source-grounded lessons with your own compatible AI agent, and optionally sync a library across devices. Local reading needs no account. The beta is free to use, with up to 50 cloud accounts and 1 GB per account. Your external AI provider may have its own costs.

Your material and your decisions

Only upload, store or share material you are authorized to use. You retain your rights to your material. Cloud storage permits PRISM and its hosting providers to process it to operate the library. Adding a PDF enables connected-agent access to selected text and page images as disclosed during import; private-source access can be revoked in the source overview. Cloud storage and lesson approvals remain separate. Do not use the service to distribute unauthorized content, attack other users, bypass access controls or evade quotas.

Check the original

AI explanations and detected PDF contents can be incomplete or wrong. Inspect citations and the original source. PRISM does not promise support for every PDF or improved learning outcomes. Review and approve lesson plans and revisions before relying on them.

Keep your originals

This is an initial beta. Availability, recovery and compatibility can change, and outages or bugs may occur. Keep independent copies of your important documents. Storage includes retained versions; reaching a limit pauses cloud transfers while local reading remains available. Abusive use may be restricted.
