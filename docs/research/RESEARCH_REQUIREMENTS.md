# Research and learning-claim requirements

Moved from the root agent instructions on 2026-09-06. These requirements apply to
research, product claims, and presentation experiments, not ordinary software maintenance.

## Evidence and attribution

- **Established:** convergent evidence, a strong review, or replicated findings.
- **Promising:** relevant studies with meaningful boundary conditions.
- **Experimental:** plausible and testable, but insufficiently validated for a product claim.
- **Speculative:** a long-range idea that must not drive near-term promises.

Distinguish direct findings from project inferences. Label preprints and small-sample
studies; quantitative research claims name the population or study context. Prefer
DOI, publisher, repository, or standards-body citations. Keep local links valid.
Generated explanations must not appear source-authored without direct grounding.

## Evaluation outcomes

For presentation-mode comparisons, record total learning time; immediate literal
and inferential comprehension; transfer/application; delayed retention at 24 hours
and seven days; pause/rewind and other control events; workload, fatigue, usability;
and generated-representation fidelity errors. Disclose missing measurements and why.
Seven-day explanation and transfer are the initial durability proof standard;
24-hour results support iteration. Exact recall also matters where precise wording,
symbols, values, definitions, or sequences are inherent to the task.

Report speed and accuracy together, including slower completion with better
comprehension or reduced comprehension with faster presentation. Display rate,
exposure, recognition, confidence, and immediate recall alone do not establish learning.
The [validation plan](../experiments/VALIDATION_PLAN.md) and
[owner trial](../experiments/OWNER_LEARNING_TRIAL.md) define current procedures.

## Product language and representations

Use semantic frame for an internal coherent instructional unit and semantic stream
for the alternate presentation. Reserve RSVP for the established paradigm or an
explicit experimental baseline. Prefer one-screen canvas to flashing when describing
that renderer. Meaningful units are phrases, clauses, propositions, equations, or
coherent visual events, not uninterrupted isolated words.

Textbooks contain definitions, prerequisites, explanations, worked examples,
equations, diagrams, tables, and exercises; preserve their relationships. A visual
must clarify structure, causality, space, change, comparison, or quantity. Adaptation
may use prior knowledge, goals, performance, behavior, preferences, and accessibility
needs; do not claim fixed visual or auditory learner types.

PRISM's ambitious new-learning-interface goal remains testable. Do not promise
twice-as-fast learning, reading without eye movements, guaranteed comprehension, or
cross-domain efficacy without appropriate preregistered evidence. See the
[project brief](../00_PROJECT_BRIEF.md) for current product and evidence boundaries.
